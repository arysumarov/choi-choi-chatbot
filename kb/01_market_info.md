# Central Farmers' Market (中環農墟) – Basic Information

Source: Kadoorie Farm and Botanic Garden (KFBG) website, Central Farmers' Market page (checked 8 Nov 2026).

## What is the Central Farmers' Market?
The Central Farmers' Market is a weekly, organic farmers' market at the Star Ferry Pier in Central, Hong Kong. Kadoorie Farm and Botanic Garden (KFBG, 嘉道理農場暨植物園) has run it since 2007. It is co-organised by KFBG and The "Star" Ferry Company Ltd.

It is a fair-trade platform that connects local organic farmers directly with consumers. Farmers get a fairer return for their hard work, and consumers can buy locally grown, organic, chemical-free greens.

The market offers real food, fresh taste and much lower CO2 emissions than imported food.

## When and where is it?
- **Day and time:** Every Sunday, 11:00 am to 5:00 pm
- **Venue:** Star Ferry Central Pier No. 7, Central, Hong Kong
- **Who can come:** Everyone is welcome. There is no limit on the number of visitors.
- **Price of entry:** There is no ticket. The market is a public event.

## What can I find at the market?
- Booths selling local organic farm produce (vegetables, fruits and other farm products)
- Earth-friendly products
- Local handicrafts

## Goals of the market
- Encourage Hong Kong citizens to pledge to reduce CO2 emissions by supporting local organic produce
- Promote the local economy, environmental protection and a simple life
- Build a fair-trade platform for consumers, social enterprises, artists and local farmers

## What happens in bad weather?
KFBG checks the weather signals at 7:00 am on the market day (Sunday).
- **Market is CANCELLED** if a Red or Black rainstorm signal, or Typhoon Signal No. 3 or above, is hoisted at 7:00 am.
- **Market goes ahead** if there is only an Amber rainstorm signal, a thunderstorm warning, or Typhoon Signal No. 1.

## Group buying: Community Supported Agriculture (CSA) scheme
KFBG runs a Community Supported Agriculture (CSA) group-buying scheme for the Central Farmers' Market (中環農墟 共同購買) from November 2026 to March 2027. Customers order online by scanning the QR code on the CSA poster, and can choose delivery or pick-up. For details, visitors should ask KFBG staff at the market or contact the enquiry hotline above.
