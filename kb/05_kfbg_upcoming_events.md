# KFBG Events and Activities – Upcoming (from November 2026)

Source: KFBG website event pages (checked 8 Nov 2026). Dates can change. For booking and details, visitors should check www.kfbg.org. Most events take place at KFBG in Tai Po, not at the Central Farmers' Market.

## Every week
- **Central Farmers' Market** – every Sunday, 11:00 am to 5:00 pm, Star Ferry Central Pier No. 7. Open to everyone.

## Food and farming
- **Community Supported Agriculture (CSA) scheme** – November 2026 to March 2027. Group buying of Central Farmers' Market produce, with delivery or pick-up; order online with the QR code on the CSA poster.
- **Herb Experience Activity 2026** – 5 December 2026. For all ages: children, students, adults and families.

## Countryside Eco-festival 2026 – "Hong Kong Forest Ecosystems" (21–22 November 2026, at KFBG)
A big weekend festival for families, students and adults. Activities include:
- Wildlife Adventure Eco-Tour for Beginners (21 and 22 Nov)
- Rescue Centre Tour (21 and 22 Nov)
- Animal Encounters and Animal Ambassadors on Exhibits (21 and 22 Nov)
- Public Talk Series (21 and 22 Nov)
- "Ripples & Pulses between the Rings" Woodcraft & Lower Farm Tree Tour (21 and 22 Nov)
- Plant Encounters (22 Nov)
- Workshops on 21 Nov: Faux Cloisonné Bird Brooch / Magnet DIY, Botanical Handcraft Accessory, Natural Antioxidant Hand Wash Scrub Soap, "Flowers of the Forest" Oil Pastel, and a Conservation Genetics Laboratory guided tour (how DNA forensics fights illegal wildlife trade)
- Workshops on 22 Nov: Bird's Nest Fern Kokedama, Orchid Planting, Nature Journaling, Owlet Figurine Painting, "Bee Close" Honey Lotion
