# KFBG Farming Courses, Fun Farm Facts and Souvenirs

Source: KFBG website, Regenerative Agriculture Department and Souvenirs & Merchandise pages (checked 8 Nov 2026). These are examples of programmes KFBG has run. Most are already finished, so for dates of future sessions visitors should check www.kfbg.org.

## Fun facts: Hong Kong grows more than vegetables!
KFBG's Regenerative Agriculture Department has run workshops showing that Hong Kong farms can produce:
- **Tea** – "Hong Kong Tea Workshop – Experiencing Local Tea Harvesting and Processing" (latest session 1 Nov 2026), and the "Hong Kong Tea Pavilion"
- **Coffee** – "Hong Kong Coffee Encounters"
- **Honey** – beekeeping courses (latest: Beekeeping Course 2026, 12 and 19 Sep 2026), "The Little Beekeeper", apiary tours and "Hive Dive – The connection between honeybees and us"
- **Fruit** – guava harvesting tours, a Fruit Forest Walk, urban fruit tree planting workshops and fruit seedling orders
- **Herbs and flowers** – herb planting workshops, Herb Experience Activity, and Chrysanthemum Planting Day

KFBG has also run a workshop on "Planting Vegetables in Synchrony with the Seasons of the Year", which teaches growing vegetables according to the season.
