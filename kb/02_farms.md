# The Farms at the Central Farmers' Market

Source: KFBG website, Central Farmers' Market page, "Farmers' Information" (checked 8 Nov 2026).

## Overview
Eight local farms are listed for the Central Farmers' Market. They all come from the New Territories of Hong Kong:
- **Yuen Long (元朗):** Puppy Organic Farm, Ka Kin Farm, Blue Girl Organic Farm, AuLaw Organic Farm, Pig Kun Farm
- **Ta Kwu Ling (打鼓嶺):** Ming Yuen Farm, Cham Shan Farm, Zen Organic Farm

Six farms are certified organic by the Hong Kong Organic Resource Centre (HKORC). Two farms are "self-claimed organic" (Ming Yuen Farm and Cham Shan Farm). See the file about organic certification for what this means.

Fun facts:
- The oldest farm is **Ming Yuen Farm**, established in 1950.
- The biggest farm is **Zen Organic Farm** (20,280 m², almost three football pitches).
- The smallest and youngest farm is **Pig Kun Farm** (570 m², started in 2019).
- **Puppy Organic Farm** has been organic the longest: since 2003, when the farm started.

The list does not say which stall each farm has at the market. To find a farm's stall, visitors can walk around the market or ask a farmer or KFBG staff on site.
