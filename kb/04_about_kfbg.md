# About Kadoorie Farm and Botanic Garden (KFBG, 嘉道理農場暨植物園)

Source: KFBG website pages on Events and Programmes, Community Projects, Nature Education, Transformative Workshops and Regenerative Agriculture (checked 8 Nov 2026).

## Who is KFBG?
Kadoorie Farm and Botanic Garden (KFBG) is a registered charity in Hong Kong (Registered Charity Number 91/04276). It organises the Central Farmers' Market.

- **Address:** Lam Kam Road, Tai Po, New Territories, Hong Kong
- **General phone:** (852) 2483 7200
- **Website:** www.kfbg.org
- **2026 is KFBG's 70th anniversary.**

## What does KFBG do?
KFBG's work includes:
- **Regenerative agriculture:** supporting local farming through the Central Farmers' Market, a Community Supported Agriculture (CSA) scheme, and courses on farming, beekeeping, herbs, tea and coffee
- **Conservation and research:** protecting Hong Kong's plants (flora) and animals (fauna)
- **Wildlife rescue:** KFBG runs a Wild Animal Rescue Centre (WARC) that rescues wild snakes, birds and mammals and releases them back to the wild
- **Nature education:** programmes for school children, families and adults
- **Sustainable living:** programmes that encourage a simple, low-consumption lifestyle

## Nature education
KFBG offers programmes for everyone, from young children to adults. They focus on people's relationship with nature and on conservation issues in Hong Kong. Most programmes must be booked in advance.

## Community projects
KFBG shares nature education with different communities across Hong Kong through outreach events, talks at schools and community centres, and special exhibitions. For example, it runs a free Sunday programme for migrant domestic workers ("helpers") at KFBG.

## KFBG's philosophy (Transformative Workshops)
KFBG believes that having lots of money and possessions does not always bring true happiness. It can lead to too much consumption and a wasteful lifestyle, which destroys natural habitats and pollutes air, water and soil. KFBG uses the idea of Gaia, the ancient Greek personification of the Earth, who should be respected like our mother because she gives us life. Its workshops help people connect deeply with nature and live in a more holistic way.

## Can I visit the farm?
Yes. KFBG is in Tai Po. Many workshops, tours and events happen there. For opening hours, tickets and directions, visitors should check www.kfbg.org or call (852) 2483 7200.
