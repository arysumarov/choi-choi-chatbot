The market農墟

Real food, straight from the farmers由農夫直接賣畀你嘅真食物

Since 2007, Kadoorie Farm and Botanic Garden (KFBG) and the Star Ferry have run a weekly organic market at Central Pier. It's a fair-trade platform: local farmers sell directly to you, so they get a fairer return, and you get fresh, locally grown greens with far fewer food miles. 由 2007 年開始，嘉道理農場暨植物園（KFBG）同天星小輪每星期喺中環碼頭搞有機農墟。呢度係一個公平貿易平台：本地農夫直接賣菜畀你，佢哋得到更公平嘅回報，你就買到新鮮本地菜，食物里程又少好多。

The people behind your vegetables種出你啲菜嘅人

👨‍🍳

Andrew

Pig Kun Farm 豬君農園 · Tai Tong, Yuen Long豬君農園 · 元朗大棠

Andrew used to be a head chef at a big Chinese restaurant. Being a farmer was his dream since he was a kid – even though as a child he really didn't like eating vegetables! His family runs the farm on rented land, and it's HKORC certified.Andrew 以前係大酒樓嘅大廚。做農夫係佢細細個嘅夢想——雖然佢細個其實好唔鍾意食菜！農場由佢屋企人喺租返嚟嘅地經營，有 HKORC 有機認證。

"Hong Kong can grow high-quality, unique and safe farm produce."「其實我哋香港都可以種出一啲好高品質、獨特同埋安全嘅農產品。」
👩‍🌾

Rose

Blue Girl Organic Farm · Yuen LongBlue Girl Organic Farm · 元朗

Rose's mum, Flora, started the farm because shop vegetables didn't taste like the ones from her childhood village. Their No. 1 best-seller for years: sweet potato leaves. They weed and catch bugs by hand – often at night.Rose 嘅媽媽 Flora 開農場，係因為街市啲菜唔似佢細個喺鄉下食嗰種味道。佢哋多年嚟賣得最好嘅係番薯葉。除草捉蟲全部靠人手，好多時仲要夜晚捉蟲。

This summer: three months of heat and rain, and almost no harvest – but rent and wages still had to be paid.今年夏天：連續三個月又熱又落雨，差唔多冇收成——但租金同人工照樣要畀。
🧑‍🤝‍🧑

A farming couple一對農夫夫婦

Kam Tin, Yuen Long元朗錦田

Their happiest moment: when customers come back to say "that was delicious!" and buy again. They're honest about worms too – organic produce can't be fully controlled, so if a melon is bad inside, they make it up to you.佢哋最開心嘅時候：客人返嚟話「好好食呀！」再幫襯。佢哋對蟲都好坦白——有機嘢控制唔到㗎，如果個瓜入面壞咗，佢哋會補返畀你。

"We just sell honestly. Farmers work really hard – it's not simple."「我哋平時靠老實賣嘢。農夫真係好辛苦，唔係咁簡單嘅。」

HKORC = Hong Kong Organic Resource Centre. Hong Kong has no law setting organic standards, so certification is voluntary. "Self-claimed" farms say they farm organically but don't hold the certificate – ask the farmer about their methods. Stall positions change, so just walk around and say hi! HKORC = 香港有機資源中心。香港冇法例規定有機標準，認證係自願嘅。「自稱有機」嘅農場話自己用有機方法耕種，但冇攞認證——想知佢哋點種，可以直接問農夫。攤位位置會變，行吓同農夫打個招呼啦！

Straight from the farmers農夫親口講

Why does organic cost more?點解有機菜貴啲？

We asked. Here's what the farmers said.我哋問咗，以下係農夫嘅答案。

👨‍🍳 Andrew · Pig Kun Farm

  1. Organic fertiliser costs much more than chemical fertiliser.有機肥料貴過化肥好多。
  2. Without chemical fertiliser, "a plant needs as many days as it needs" – slower growth, lower yields.唔用化肥，「佢需要幾多日生長就要幾多日」——生得慢，產量少。
  3. Bugs! Natural sprays from plant extracts don't stop most insects, so a lot of the crop is lost.蟲！植物提煉嘅天然農藥對大部分蟲都冇乜用，所以損耗好大。
"We sell at a higher price, but we don't actually earn more money."「賣貴咗，亦都冇賺多咗錢，其實。」

👩‍🌾 Rose · Blue Girl Organic Farm

  1. Hong Kong land rent is expensive – the farm rents its land.香港地租好貴——農場嘅地係租返嚟。
  2. High wages, and hiring foreign workers because few locals want to farm.人工貴，而且本地少人做農夫，要請外勞。
  3. No weedkiller means weeding and catching bugs by hand – often at night.唔用除草劑，就要人手除草捉蟲——好多時夜晚都要捉。
  4. Small fields are hard to work with big machines.田細，大型機械好難用。
She feels the price is fair for what you get.佢覺得個價錢對得住啲菜。

About this project關於呢個計劃

Closing the gap between farmers and young people拉近農夫同年輕人嘅距離

Choi Choi was made by Group 2 of ITS2028 Global Challenges II at Hong Kong Baptist University. Most market customers are over 40, and farmers told us they'd love more young people to know about local farming. Choi Choi is our way of helping young visitors start a conversation.菜菜係香港浸會大學 ITS2028 Global Challenges II 第二組嘅作品。農墟大部分客人都係 40 歲以上，農夫話好想多啲年輕人認識本地農業。菜菜就係我哋幫年輕人同農夫打開話匣子嘅方法。
